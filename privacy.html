Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Locally (“we”, “us”) is an online rental marketplace for Zimbabwe, available at locally.co.zw and as an Android app. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email Google shares with us.
  • Viewing requests: your name, email, phone number, preferred date and time, and any notes you add.
  • Rental requests: your name, email, phone number, preferred area, budget, number of bedrooms, timeline and notes.
  • Property submissions: your name, email, phone number, the property’s address, rent, details, description and photos.
  • Location: only if you tap the location button. Your location is used on your device to sort listings by distance. We do not store it.

How we use your information

To show listings, to arrange viewings, to match tenants with properties, to contact you about your requests, to review property submissions, and to keep Locally safe.

Who handles your data

  • Supabase stores accounts, requests, listings and photos.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google provides sign-in, if you choose it, and the fonts used in the app.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information. We share your contact details only with the broker who handles your request.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, including secure connections (HTTPS) and access limits on our database. No online service is completely risk-free.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Locally is not meant for people under 18.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when something changes and show the new date at the top.
